# Flat battery and outdoor display sourcing — V102

Research snapshot 2026-11-05. These are candidates, not substitutions or a release BOM. Prices are USD per unit, before shipping, tax, tariffs, connectors and assembly. Stock changes. Existing V101 $11.67 lead is EastRising ER-TFT018-3 ($2.18) plus US Electronics USE-18650-2600PCBJST ($9.49); that 200 cd/m² display is described by the seller as not sunlight-readable, and the 19 mm diameter cell requires an enclosure redesign. Neither is power-qualified. See ../sourcing-v101/README.md.

## Flat protected packs

* **US Electronics USE-104060-3K-PCBJST** — 3 Ah nominal, 62 × 40.5 × 10 mm maximum; PCM G3J+8205*2; 153 ± 3 mm AWG26 red/black leads with JST-PH-2P connector. Manufacturer specification states **0.5C maximum continuous discharge (1.5 A nominal-capacity basis)**, 3 V discharge end, and ≤150 mΩ new-cell 1 kHz AC impedance. Do not turn AC impedance into cold/aged transient resistance. The catalog lists 0.6 A discharge, consistent with its 0.2C standard condition, not the 1.5 A maximum. $14.95 at 1, $14.49 at 10, $13.65 at 100; 1,247 reported stock, marketplace seller; shipping may add cost. The prior conditional ~2.75 A input operating case exceeds 1.5 A if continuous, but no conclusion about a particular brief pulse follows without its timing and PCM limits. Not qualified for the phone. Check mating socket polarity and retention, not just PH pitch.
  * [Manufacturer pack specification](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/5336/USE-104060-3KPCBJST.pdf); [US distributor, prices and stock](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/us-electronics-inc/USE-104060-3K-PCBJST/15781481).
* **Jauch LP705176JS+PCM+2 WIRES 70MM / 246523** — 3 Ah minimum; distributor describes 78 × 51.8 × 7.8 mm, two wire leads (connector must be selected and validated). Distributor lists $21.59 at 1, $17.953 at 10, 197 stock; 28-week standard lead time. An older manufacturer-authored 11/2018 pack sheet documents overcurrent range 5.5–9.5 A, and a 10 A maximum for 2–3 ms, **not a 10 A continuous rating**. Its terms are descriptive and request latest data; no continuous maximum, cold/aged pack impedance or pulse duration matching the modem were established. Distributor links a generic cell sheet rather than that pack sheet. Confirm exact current revision and limits with Jauch before qualification. [Distributor](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/jauch-quartz/LP705176JS-PCM-2-WIRES-70MM/9560997); [older pack sheet](https://www.repcomsrl.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/lp705176js_1s1p_2_wire_70mm.pdf).
* **Adafruit 2011** — $12.50 at 1, $11.25 at 10, $10 at 100; 60 × 36 × 7 mm vendor listing, 2 Ah nominal, protected, genuine two-pin JST-PH. Linked PKCELL LP-803860-with-PCM sheet gives 1C = 2 A maximum continuous, while the seller page's stated dimensions differ from that sheet's cell designation: verify the shipped revision and full pack/cable dimensions. It does not establish cold/aged impedance or modem-duration pulse behavior. It cannot support the conditional 2.75 A case as a continuous current on the linked rating. [Seller](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2011); [linked manufacturer sheet](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/datasheets/LiIon2000mAh37V.pdf).

The existing modeled Jauch 246524 maximum battery envelope is 78.5 × 59.5 × 7.9 mm. In an axis-aligned bounding-box comparison only, 246523's listed body fits by 0.5 × 7.7 × 0.1 mm; such margins are not usable installation clearance, and its post-cycle expansion, leads, connector, retention, tolerances and thermal sensor remain unresolved. The USE 104060 body is 2.1 mm thicker than that reservation. Adafruit's listed body fits this box but actual swelling and connector allowance are unverified. None is a verified assembly fit.

## Brighter display leads

* **Winstar WF18HSLAADNN0** — manufacturer specifies 128 × 160, ST7735S or equivalent, SPI, transmissive, 900 cd/m² typical, 34.0 × 45.83 × 2.65 mm, 2.5–3.7 V (2.75 V typical). This is 4.5 times the nominal 200 cd/m² of the low-price lead, not a guarantee of outdoor readability: cover glass, glare, viewing angle and actual illumination need a sample test. The manufacturer uses inquiry pricing; no verified unit/quantity US price or stock was found. Its 2.65 mm listed thickness is 0.25 mm greater than the prior Newhaven 2.4 mm nominal, before tolerance and case materials. Exact FPC contact orientation, pad mapping, mating connector, backlight limits and integration have not been verified; do not reuse the Newhaven connector merely because SPI and size are similar. [Manufacturer specifications and inquiry](https://www.winstar.com.tw/products/tft-lcd/high-brightness-tft/1-8-tft-module-128x160.html).
* **Maclight MLT018A24-10** — maker advertises 1000 cd/m², 128 × 160, ILI9163V, 34 × 47 × 2.4 mm and SPI/MCU/RGB. Price, US availability, connector and exact interface selection remain unverified. Similar outline is not evidence of mating or fit. [Manufacturer page](https://www.szmaclight.com/product/1-8-sunlight-readable-tft-128x160-1000-nit-high-brightness.html).

For comparison, the existing Newhaven NHD-1.8-128160EF-SSXN-F was $18.89 in the prior check; explicitly recommended connector Molex 0524352471 is a separate cost and must be sourced and validated per the existing report. Combining it with the $14.95 flat pack would be $33.84 for just these two parts, but **the pack fails the conditional continuous-current comparison**. Combining it with Jauch 246523 is $40.48 before its connector, but is electrically unqualified. Neither establishes an affordable, working phone. Bright Winstar and Maclight combinations cannot be priced until actual quotes and shipping are obtained.

## Evidence needed before selecting

1. Battery manufacturer: full-pack cold and aged DC pulse resistance or guaranteed voltage droop at relevant state of charge, current waveform and temperature; PCM trip limits and time curves, continuous-current limits, leads/connector polarity and resistance, swelling, accessible temperature sensor, and charging limits for exact revision.
2. Display manufacturer/distributor: quote at 1/10/100 shipped to US; current FPC drawing, top/bottom contacts, thickness and pitch tolerances, explicit compatible connector MPN, pin mapping, backlight voltage/current limits, optical specifications with cover lens, and sample availability.
3. Rebuild the mechanical stack and test real parts, thermal charging interlock, power transients, RF/carrier acceptance, stencil and fabrication process. Country/carrier selection remains open. No hardware was ordered or changed.
