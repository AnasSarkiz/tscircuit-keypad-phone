Exact protected2.6Ah 3.7V cylindrical pack with leads and connector. DigiKey Marketplace lists916, $9.49 at1, $9.19 at10, $8.75 at100, marked non-cancelable/non-returnable and shipped by US Electronics: https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/us-electronics-inc/USE-18650-2600PCBJST/15781482. Manufacturer spec: https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/5335/USE-18650-2600PCBJST.pdf . Its19mm diameter and68.5mm length max (datasheet) are markedly thicker than the prior flat Jauch candidate (7.9mm after cycling); this requires a new case/battery-retainer study and adds capacity/runtime tradeoffs. Vendor product summary elsewhere lists69.5mm length, so obtain exact pack drawing/sample before fixing the envelope.

The manufacturer sheet gives2.6A maximum continuous discharge, charge0–45°C, and lists PCM overcurrent4–12A,10–20ms at25°C. The sheet's 1kHz AC impedance acceptance≤150mΩ at23±2°C after standard charge is **not** an upper bound for cold/aged DC pulse resistance. Its PCM table has a50mΩ impedance field at25°C; text extraction does not unambiguously establish its min/typ/max or worst-case over temperature, and it excludes cell/leads. The earlier illustrative LTE total-input estimate2.75A exceeds the stated2.6A continuous limit, but an instantaneous/pulsed waveform is not the same as continuous operation; actual peak duration, mode, protection delay and safe cell/connector current remain unverified. No claim that this pack passes or fails a specific modem pulse without its waveform. Connector polarity/current and the complete current path must be checked. A high-capacitance bulk reservoir does not substitute for protection and source qualification.

Other low-cost comparison: Adafruit1781 2200mAh with protection,69mm×18mm diameter, $9.95 (10–99 $8.96,100+ $7.96), https://www.adafruit.com/product/1781. A new harness cannot be assumed safe or approved merely to remove that limit.

## Cost implication and decision gates

At the observed one-unit part prices, ER-TFT018-3 + USE-18650-2600PCBJST sum to **$11.67**, versus $45.73 for the previous two candidates; `check-comparison.py` verifies arithmetic. This excludes the new connector, both shipments, tax/tariff, minimum-order effects, PCB redesign, enclosure, modem, assembly and tests. It is a sourcing lead, **not a complete BOM quote or a qualified phone design**.

Before selecting the lower-cost pair: decide acceptable outdoor readability and case thickness; determine carrier/exact modem SKU and waveform; obtain guaranteed low-SOC/cold/aged protected-pack pulse impedance, PCM trip/delay and connector/wire ratings; obtain ER-TFT018-3 exact pinout/FPC/connector and display tolerances; then model fit, backlight and firmware, and sample/test. No assumptions silently migrated into PCB. Prior safety and manufacturing blockers remain; both MCU PRs stay open. Source references are links only; no vendor documents copied.
